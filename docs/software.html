Fourteen applications for editing and generating

A filmmaker's notes on where editing tools are going. They rest only on what each publisher makes public: documentation, repository, pricing page, dated announcement. This is not a benchmark: nothing here is measured. Prices change quickly, so check before you decide.

Final Cut Pro

Editing software · By Apple

What it does
Video editing on Mac and iPad, with audio, titles, effects and colour. Version 12.4 dates from 29/09/2026.
Strengths
The Magnetic Timeline keeps clips in sync as you move and trim them. Magnetic Mask, automatic captions, third-party effects through FxPlug, exchange through FCPXML.
Limits
Mac and iPad only. On Mac, macOS 15.6 or later; some features need Apple silicon, others a connection. Some premium content is for subscribers only. Apple's October 2026 announcement names no MCP server for Final Cut.
Who it is for
Solo editors and small teams already on Mac.
Licence
Proprietary
Price
One-time purchase $299.99, or Apple Creator Studio at $12.99 a month or $129 a year (30-day trial).

DaVinci Resolve

Editing and finishing software · By Blackmagic Design

What it does
Editing, colour, effects, audio and delivery in one application, organised into pages. Version 21.1.2 in October 2026.
Strengths
Colour and finishing in the same place as the edit. A very complete free version. Mac, Windows, Linux. Native MCP server since 21.1 (Studio).
Limits
The free version stops at Ultra HD 60 fps and 8-bit formats. The AI engine, 10-bit and, since 21.1, Python scripting are Studio only. The screen shows a lot at once.
Who it is for
From the beginner to feature film post-production.
Licence
Proprietary
Price
Free. Studio: one-time purchase, shown at $236 on sale instead of $295.

Palmier Pro

Editor with generation · By Palmier, Inc.

What it does
Multi-track editing on Mac, with image and video generation inside the timeline. External agents work on the same project through MCP. Version 0.12.1 of 02/11/2026.
Strengths
The editor and MCP are free, with no account. Local transcription and footage search. Timeline export as XML to another editor.
Limits
macOS 26 on Apple silicon only. Source is published only through v0.7.6: later releases are proprietary, and the repository keeps only the old code and the binaries. Generation goes through the cloud and credits.
Who it is for
Editors on a recent Mac who want to work with an agent.
Licence
GPL-3.0 through v0.7.6, proprietary after
Price
Free. Pro $29 a month (5,000 credits). Max $69 a month (12,000 credits). Shown on sale, instead of $49 and $99.

Diffusion Studio

Video editor for agents · By Diffusion Studio Inc.

What it does
An editor where every edit is written as code: change the canvas and the code follows, change the code and the canvas redraws. A command-line agent cuts, captions and renders in it. Version 0.216.0 of 18/10/2026.
Strengths
Open source, with a free editor and exports. Canvas and timeline in one tool. Fine animation curves, suited to motion design. Headless mode.
Limits
Desktop on macOS (M1 or later) and Windows x64. The publisher also announces a browser version, not verified here. Brand assets are outside the licence. Plan details not re-read.
Who it is for
People making short edits or motion design with a coding agent.
Licence
MPL-2.0
Price
Free. Paid credits for AI models run by the service.

Velorn

Editing and generation workstation · By VelornLabs

What it does
A desktop application that brings together shot planning, generation through ComfyUI, timeline, captions and export. Version 0.3.37 of 12/10/2026.
Strengths
Open source. Windows, Mac and Linux. Runs your own ComfyUI graphs. A music video mode, from the song to the timeline. An MCP server where most writes go through a preview.
Limits
All generation needs a separately installed ComfyUI on the same machine. Heavy video workflows may need 24 GB of VRAM or more. According to the README, imports, exports and generated files cannot all be undone.
Who it is for
People who already know ComfyUI and want to finish a film in one place.
Licence
GPL-3.0
Price
Free. Cloud workflows depend on provider credits or keys.

07/11/2026

Martini

Online AI production workspace · By C47, Inc.

What it does
An infinite canvas for developing shots with several models, then a timeline for cutting the selected takes. The work is shared, in the cloud.
Strengths
3D camera control: position, lens, movement. A history of each shot (prompt, model, references). XML export to Premiere Pro, Resolve, Final Cut Pro and CapCut. MCP, CLI and API.
Limits
Online service, with no local version announced. The free plan has public projects only. To finish a film in Premiere or Resolve, the documentation points to XML export. Speed is the publisher's claim, not measured.
Who it is for
Directors and studios making fully generated films, as a team.
Licence
Proprietary, commercial licence on every plan
Price
Indie free (pay-as-you-go credits). Pro $30 a month. Studio $150 a month. 1 credit ("olive") is $0.10. Your own fal key is possible.

Dreamina

Generation platform · By CapCut

What it does
Generation of images, videos and talking avatars from text, photos or audio, on CapCut's site.
Strengths
Seedance and Seedream in one place. Free daily credits to try it. Very low entry price.
Limits
No local version announced. It is not primarily an editing application. The publisher asks you to re-read its terms before commercial use. The price outside the promotion was not recorded.
Who it is for
People who want to generate short shots quickly or try these models.
Licence
Proprietary
Price
Free option. First-month offer at $0.29, announced until 9 November. Basic, Standard and Advanced plans.

Higgsfield

Generation platform and canvas · By Higgsfield

What it does
Image and video generation with many models, a node canvas (Canvas), and plug-ins for DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, After Effects, Photoshop, Figma and Blender.
Strengths
Many models under one account. Reusable characters (Soul ID). A live shared canvas and reusable workflow templates. In Resolve, seven tools in one panel.
Limits
Runs on the publisher's servers: the Resolve plug-in needs a connection. Unlimited offers do not apply to Canvas, MCP or the CLI. Subscription credits do not roll over.
Who it is for
Advertising, social media, and editors who want to generate from inside their software.
Licence
Proprietary
Price
Limited free tier. Starter $19, Plus $47, Ultra $99 a month, billed annually.

FLORA

Creative canvas · By FLORA

What it does
A canvas where text, image, video and audio are connected nodes, with more than 50 models. Since 07/07/2026, a Timeline node sequences, trims and stitches without leaving the canvas.
Strengths
Live collaboration. One budget shared by the team, at published model rates. Batch variations. API and MCP from Starter.
Limits
Online service, with no local version announced. The free plan: one seat, three projects, text and image models. The Timeline is a node on the canvas, not a separate editing application. Export formats not re-read.
Who it is for
Agencies and creative teams.
Licence
Proprietary
Price
Free $0. Starter $18, Pro $50, Max $200 per seat per month.

Krea

Generation platform and node graph · By Krea

What it does
Image, video and 3D generation, LoRA training, and Nodes, an editor that links models and tools into automated chains that can become apps.
Strengths
A very wide range of video and image models under one account. Real-time generation. Reusable, shareable apps built from workflows.
Limits
Online service. Not an editing application. Access to video models is limited on the first plans, and the commercial licence depends on the plan: check the publisher's table.
Who it is for
Designers and small studios who want to try many models and automate.
Licence
Proprietary
Price
Free, pay as you go. Basic $5.25, Pro $21, Max $63 a month on the pricing page as recorded (annual billing). Business for teams.

Figma Weave

Node graph for image and video · By Figma

What it does
Formerly Weavy. Connected nodes turn an image or a prompt into a finished result, with branches to try several directions and direct editing tools.
Strengths
Steps stay visible and you can return to them. Layer compositor, colour, levels, crop, clip trim. A workflow can become a simple tool for others. Commercial licence from the free plan.
Limits
Online service. Subscription separate from Figma. No video models or sharing on the free plan. You can trim a clip, but no timeline is announced in the pages read.
Who it is for
Design and campaign teams adapting visuals across formats.
Licence
Proprietary
Price
Free $0 (150 credits). Starter $24 (1,500), Professional $45 (4,000), Max $100 (10,000) a month. Annually: $202, $378, $840.

Maestro

Local generation and editing studio · By Blizaine, d'après WanGP

What it does
A fully local studio for video, image and music. Director mode plans a music video or a short film from a prompt, Editor cuts it on several tracks. Version 4.0.0 of 04/11/2026.
Strengths
Generation on your own machine. Automatic tuning for the hardware. Install through Pinokio. A clip on the timeline can go back to generation and return as a new take. Built-in MCP.
Limits
Windows or Linux with an NVIDIA card: no Mac or AMD for now. At least 150 GB free, 500 GB for all models. Some models have their own restrictive licence.
Who it is for
People with a large NVIDIA card who want to keep everything at home.
Licence
WanGP Non-Commercial Evaluation License 1.1. Outputs can be used commercially with attribution; commercial use of the software needs a licence.
Price
Free for non-commercial use.

07/11/2026

Phosphene

Local generation studio for Mac · By Mr Bizarro

What it does
Local video generation on Apple silicon, with sound, lip-sync and character training. One Shot, Storyboard and an editor for cutting. Version 5.0.1 of 06/11/2026.
Strengths
No cloud, no key, no NVIDIA card. Every current feature stays free according to the author. The timeline can be sent to Resolve, Premiere Pro or After Effects. MCP and command line.
Limits
Apple silicon Macs only. What you can do depends on memory: 16 GB for LTX at small sizes, 36 GB for MiniMax H3. Anonymous analytics are on by default and can be turned off in settings. Check the models: the README says the Hailuo H3 licence excludes the EU, UK, South Korea and US.
Who it is for
People who want to generate on their Mac with no subscription.
Licence
MIT for the code. Each model has its own licence.
Price
Free. A Pro offer is announced at $15 a month or $120 a year.

07/11/2026

Draw Things

Local generator for Apple devices · By Draw Things

What it does
Image generation, and video depending on the model, on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Infinite canvas, local LoRA training, optional compute on the publisher's servers.
Strengths
Private, free generation on the device. The publisher releases its own speed comparisons on Apple silicon. The gRPC server is open source and can run on another machine.
Limits
Not an editing application. Speed figures come from the publisher. The Draw Things+ price was not recorded.
Who it is for
People who want to generate on their Mac or iPad, with no command line.
Licence
GPL-3.0 for the community repository. Other licences on request from the publisher.
Price
Free. Community $8.99 a month (cloud compute). Draw Things+ for more compute.
